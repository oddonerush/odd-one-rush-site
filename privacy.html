Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

About This Application

ODD ONE RUSH Uploader is a private application used by the channel owner to upload, schedule, and manage videos on their own YouTube channel.

Information Accessed

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to access the channel owner's YouTube account and the YouTube Data API. Depending on granted permissions, it may access video metadata, upload status, scheduling information, and channel information.

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to upload videos, schedule publication, verify video status, prevent duplicate uploads, and manage local video files.

Storage and Sharing

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally in protected form. Operational records may be stored locally. Information is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Retention and Deletion

Local video files may be deleted after publication has been confirmed and the configured retention period has passed. Operational logs may be retained for duplicate prevention and auditing.

Access Revocation

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application operator through the contact email provided on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
